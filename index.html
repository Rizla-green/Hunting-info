Options

Dropbox auto-backup

Connect once and the app backs itself up to Dropbox every 7 days (it checks when you open it) — nothing to paste, nothing to renew. It keeps the newest 12 backups and tidies the older ones. Do this once on each phone or browser you use. "Back up now" makes a backup straight away.

Advanced: paste a token instead

A token pasted here expires after a few hours, so backups stop. Only use this if Connect Dropbox doesn't work for you.

Import data

Import an Excel sheet of past records into any category. Works for a fresh category with no entries yet — pick the category, map your sheet's columns to the app's fields, and preview before importing. Photos embedded in the sheet (e.g. Fox's right/left/group photos) are picked up automatically from whichever row they sit on.

Match existing entries to properties and fields

For entries logged before you drew your farms and fields, added new land, or imported from a spreadsheet. First it finds entries still on "Other" that sit inside one of your farm outlines and asks before moving them to that farm (an entry that already has a farm is never changed). Then it goes through every Deer, Fox, Squirrel, Boar and Goats entry with no Field set and puts it in the field its map position falls in. Entries that only have a what3words get their position looked up first (one lookup each, using your what3words allowance), which also puts them on the shot maps. It never changes a field you've already set. Do this after drawing the fields and after importing — and it's safe to run again.

Export

Export the full dataset, or open a section and use its own export button for just that species/area.
